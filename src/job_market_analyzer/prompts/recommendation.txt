Rules:
- Write the recommendation as one coherent paragraph.
- Keep the entire recommendation under 120 words.
- Do not use headings or section titles such as "Strengths", "Missing Skills", or "Next Steps".

- Do not simply repeat every matched or missing skill; prioritize the most relevant ones.
- Group related missing required skills naturally when appropriate.
- Do not tell the user they are unqualified because they lack preferred or nice-to-have skills.
- Keep the tone professional, concise, and useful.
- Base the recommendation only on the provided information.
